Meet The Conveners

Bada Muhammad Aluthaimeen

Convener, Africa Disrupt Summit 1.0

Bada Muhammad Aluthaimeen — known as "Badabytes" — is not in the business of hype; he's in the business of building. A Digital Marketer, Strategist, and Entrepreneur, he is obsessed with tearing down the culture of shortcuts and replacing it with systems that actually work.

As the Founder & CEO of Al-Ameen Spark, he architects digital ecosystems that turn brands, startups, and communities into growth machines — strategies that don't just look good on slides but deliver results that can't be ignored.

Odedeyi Nafisat

Co-Convener, Africa Disrupt Summit 1.0

Odedeyi Nafisat is the Chief Operating Officer of Al-Ameen Spark, where she drives execution, systems, and team alignment to turn bold strategies into tangible results.

Dr. Osagie Bill Aigbogun

Finance and Strategic Leadership: Driving Africa's Next Wave of Disruption

Dr. Osagie Bill Aigbogun (FCCA) is an accountant with over fifteen years experience in practice and industry. He is a chartered accountant with the ACCA holding a fellow member position with the institute.
